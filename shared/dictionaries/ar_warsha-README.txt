القاموس العربي للتدقيق الإملائي في «سطر» — Arabic spelling dictionary for Satr

Source: Ayaspell, the Arabic dictionary for Hunspell (Mohamed Kebdani, Taha Zerrouki),
as packaged in LibreOffice's dictionaries repository (ar/ar.aff, ar/ar.dic).
License: GPL 2.0 / LGPL 2.1 / MPL 1.1 tri-license (see ar_warsha-COPYING.txt and ar_warsha-AUTHORS.txt).

Converted for Satr's spelling engine by Warsha's tools/convert_ar_dict.py: each two-letter flag
became one character, the AF aliases were expanded, comments and morphology fields were dropped,
and the IGNORE (harakat) and ICONV (alef wasla, lam-alef) rules are applied by Satr itself.
The words and affix rules are unchanged.
